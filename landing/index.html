Why Vellum

Out of the way until you need it. Then it is right there.

Markdown, rendered live

Type #, -, - [ ], **bold** or ``` and it renders in place — powered by Muya, the editor engine of MarkText.

Checklists that behave

Enter continues the list, Tab and ⇧Tab nest it. Paste a list from anywhere and it stays a list — even with a Chinese input method.

Just files

Each note is a .md file in a folder you choose. Open them in Obsidian, sync them with iCloud, grep them. No lock-in.

When and where

Every note remembers when it started and the city you were in. Stamp an end time when you are done.

Jump between notes

⌘K opens a switcher with every note and its date. Arrow keys, return, done.

Glass, your way

A translucent window that floats above your work. Pick a theme colour, and a sans or serif typeface.

Follows your system

Light, dark, or whatever your system is set to right now. Switch at any time in the settings.

Write like plain text

What you type on the left is what you see on the right.
